# STATUS

Last updated: 2026-11-25.

## State: published, closed

EMBERWATCH is a finished, free browser game. No further changes are planned. Bug reports are still welcome as GitHub issues or itch comments.

## Where it lives

- Play (direct page, most comfortable): https://maficonstruct.github.io/emberwatch/
- itch.io page: https://maficonstruct.itch.io/emberwatch
- Source (this repo): https://github.com/mafiConstruct/emberwatch
- Code license: MIT. Audiovisual work is procedurally generated at runtime, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (see `README.md` and `LICENSE`).

## What shipped

- One night, one fire, two entities (falling wood, frost moths). Three minutes to dawn.
- Keyboard and touch controls, mute, restart, rank at dawn (S/A/B/C).
- Reproducible runs with `?seed=N`.
- itch page with cover, description, "More information" and a devlog, published 2026-11-24.

## Known limits

- One scene only. Balance was checked against scripted policies, not against many human players.
- Touch controls are best in landscape.
